EUA impõem tarifa de 12,5% ao Brasil, até 37,5% com a anterior de 25%

Em 23 de julho de 2026, os EUA impuseram tarifas a 60 economias, 12,5% ao Brasil. Com a tarifa de 25% anunciada antes, chega a 37,5% em alguns produtos.

Fonte oficial

USTR (Escritório do Representante Comercial dos EUA), Notice of Actions in Section 301 Investigations (forced labor), Federal Register Notice de 23/07/2026

8. Brazil: Determination of Action in Investigation Based on the findings in the investigation of Brazil, considering the public comments, testimony, and the advice of the Section 301 Committee, as well as the advice of advisory committees, and in accordance with the specific direction of the President, the Trade Representative has determined to impose 12.5 percent tariffs on products of Brazil, except as provided in Annex I and Annex II, Part A, of this Notice.
